Concept 3 + free Blubird-like fonts

Same Build-Once Blocks mark. Below are free commercial-friendly fonts that get close to Blubird’s contemporary grotesque energy without buying it.

Closest overall: Bricolage Grotesque (ink traps + expressive grotesque)
Most display / fashion: Clash Display (tight, bold, high-impact)
Cleaner product brand: Cabinet Grotesk or Satoshi

Free on Google Fonts. Closest “Blubird DNA”: contemporary grotesque with ink-trap personality. Best first pick for ImagineMōde.
