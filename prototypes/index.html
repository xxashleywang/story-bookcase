Story Bookcase planner

Five takes on the same job: decide which way each shelf faces on the Herman Miller Story Bookcase (Standard or Tall) before you assemble it. Every prototype shares the same link format, so a design copied from one opens in any other.

01

Compass

Front view plus a big top-down compass. Pick a shelf, tap where it points. Keyboard friendly.
02

Blueprint

Technical isometric drawing, numbered build list with 90° rotate buttons, four camera angles, and a tip-over estimate.
03

Catalog

Editorial layout. Tap shelves to turn them, preview with books, and save a shortlist to compare side by side.
04

Pocket

Phone-first. D-pad per shelf, swipe to spin, and a step-by-step Build mode to keep open while assembling.
05

Studio

Dark 3D studio. Drag to orbit, click shelves in 3D, and plan up to four bookcases together.
